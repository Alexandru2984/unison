THE POSSIBILITY LAB

Idea Evolution: a garden for your next idea.

Plant a thought on a shared public board, then explore what it can become. Eight pure Unison functions expand, rephrase, amplify, synthesize, question, twist, headline or flip your text into reproducible branches, and you can preview every result before you plant it.

Explore ideas from every angle

Follow the living graph or browse the list. Discover trending and unexplored ideas, water the ones you like, replay how the garden grew, save favorites on your device and share any idea through its own page or image card.

Published ideas are public. Drafts, bookmarks and your list of planted ideas stay in your browser.
